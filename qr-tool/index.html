Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a QR Code?

A QR Code (Quick Response) is a two-dimensional barcode that can store information in the form of text, URLs, contact information, geographic locations and much more. QR codes can be scanned using smartphones or QR code scanners.

Can I create QR codes for free?

Yes, our QR code generator tool is completely free. You can create as many QR codes as you like without any fees.

Will my QR codes expire?

QR codes generated from this website never expire. However, if it points to a URL and that URL is deleted or changed, the QR code will no longer work.

Can I customize the color of QR codes?

Yes, you can customize the color of QR codes using the color picker in the QR code generation section.

How do I scan QR codes?

You can use the camera app on your smartphone or download a QR code scanning app from the app store. Many modern smartphones already have built-in QR code scanning functionality in their camera apps.
